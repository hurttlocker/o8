# Founder edition — positioning dossier (for the front-end agent)

**Purpose:** how to message the **managed-inference speed** benefit on the founder side (o8.run/founding) — and the same frame the future subscription rides on. Claims are backed by the internal sweep (`founding-launch-checklist.md` §F); **do NOT reveal HOW** (no model names, no architecture). Match the o8 / Marquise voice: plain-spoken, concrete, a clear stance — **no hype, no emojis**.

## The whole frame in two lines
- **Free forever with your own keys/CLI** — the whole app, the moats, the canvas. Not a trial, not crippled.
- **Founder (and later, subscription) = we run the FAST, managed version of the inference for you** — included for life (founders) / on your plan (subscription). No key to bring, no model to install, no wait.

> One-liner: **"Monetize cost, not capability."** Free if you bring your own keys; pay only if you want us to run the fast version for you.

## The speed claim (backed, but say the benefit — not the how)
- **The Brain answers your codebase instantly.** Well under a second on our managed inference — vs the 15–30-second wait you'd get running it yourself on your own CLI. Public framing: *"answers land the moment you ask"* / *"~30–60× faster than doing it yourself."* (Internal backing: 0.48s managed vs 15–30s CLI — **don't publish the raw numbers**, publish the feeling.)
- **Dictation keeps up with you.** Speak and it's there, clean — transcription + cleanup in about a second, no lag. (Internal: STT 1.09s, polish 0.37s.)
- The benefit to sell = **speed + zero setup** ("our inference makes it this much faster, with no key and nothing to install"). Never name the models, providers, or the proxy.

## What founders get (customer-facing perk list)
- **Instant Brain answers** — the managed speed tier, on automatically.
- **Premium dictation** — transcription + polish, no key needed.
- **Early access** (experimental always on), the **exclusive theme**, **your name on the founders wall** *(already in the live founder copy — keep)*.
- **Included for life, within fair use** — you never pay the future infra tier. (Say "within fair use," **never** "unlimited.")

## The subscription tie-in (for later)
The future plan = the **same fast managed inference, monthly**, for people who don't found. Founders just got it **for life, before it cost anything.** Same engine, same framing.

## Guardrails (do not break)
1. **Never reveal HOW** — no model names (flash-lite/Whisper/Gemini/etc.), no "OpenRouter"/"proxy"/architecture. Say *"our managed inference"* / *"the managed speed tier."*
2. **Never say "unlimited"** — it's fair-use-capped. Say *"on for you, within fair use."*
3. **The latencies are claims to feel, not specs to publish** — "instant" / "the moment you ask" / "~30–60× faster than running it yourself." Raw numbers stay in the internal tracker.
4. **Voice:** plain, concrete, a stance — no hype, no emojis, no hashtags.
